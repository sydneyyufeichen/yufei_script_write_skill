## Spoken Script

If you think eating clean is all it takes to be healthy, honestly, that's just level one in China. Chinese medicine looks beyond the ingredients. It also pays attention to how much you eat, when you eat, and whether your food is cold or served warm and cooked. I'm a Chinese medicine apprentice in China, and here are four habits Chinese people often avoid.

Don't eat past comfortable fullness. Food is meant to nourish you, but eating too much puts extra strain on your stomach. Have a satisfying breakfast, eat well at lunch, and keep dinner light, sometimes stopping when you're only about half full. In this framework, that may be better for your spleen and stomach. Simply eating less is an important way to care for your stomach.

Don't make cold foods and drinks an everyday habit. Even in Japan, people don't drink iced matcha every day. They usually choose it warm because matcha itself is considered cooling. Cold drinks can weaken stomach qi and slow digestion. Many Chinese people skip ice water altogether and choose warm or room-temperature water instead.

Don't eat too late at night. Your stomach needs time to rest too. Eating late forces your digestive system to keep working when it would normally be repairing and resting.

Don't eat too many fried, spicy, cold, overly sweet, or greasy foods. When they show up too often, digestion may start to feel heavier.

Follow for more simple Chinese medicine ways to understand everyday digestion.

## Chinese Translation

如果你觉得只要吃得干净就等于健康，说实话，在中国这只算第一层。中医看消化，不只看食材，还会看你吃多少、什么时候吃，以及食物是寒凉还是熟热。我正在中国学习中医师承，下面说说中国人经常避免的四个习惯。

不要吃到超过舒服的饱腹感。食物原本是用来滋养身体的，但吃得过多会让胃的负担变重。早饭吃饱、午饭吃好，晚饭吃少甚至只吃五分饱，可能对你的脾胃会更有益处，少吃是非常重要的养胃方式。

不要经常吃寒凉的食物、喝冷饮。就连日本人也不会每天都喝冰抹茶，通常会选择温热的抹茶，因为抹茶本身偏凉。冷饮会削弱胃气、减慢消化。很多中国人会完全避开冰水，选择温水或常温水。

不要太晚吃东西。胃也需要停下来休息，深夜进食会迫使消化系统在本该修复和休息的时候继续工作。

不要吃太多油炸、辛辣刺激、寒凉、甜腻的食物。这些食物出现得太频繁，可能会让消化感觉更沉重。

关注我，我会继续用简单的中医视角解释日常消化问题。
