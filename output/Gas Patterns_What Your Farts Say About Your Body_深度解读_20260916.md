## Spoken Script

Your farts may know more about your body than you do. Their smell, sound, heat, and timing can point to very different patterns. I’m studying Chinese medicine in China, so let’s figure out which one sounds most like you.

If you pass gas often but it barely smells, and you also feel bloated, TCM may connect that pattern with constrained Liver qi affecting digestion. This often shows up when you feel stressed or frustrated because the Liver is linked with emotions in TCM. You might also feel irritable, anxious, or low, along with a bitter taste or poor sleep.

Another pattern is that the gas is loud, smells strong, and may feel hot. In TCM, this points to damp-heat. Food may stay in the gut too long, like food left in a warm pot, giving bacteria more time to break it down and produce stronger-smelling gas. You may also notice mouth sores, swollen gums, fatigue, oily skin, yellow urine, bloating, constipation, or loose stools.

Then there’s gas that is almost silent but smells terrible. TCM links this combination with weak digestion and food stagnation, like a traffic jam after a meal. You may feel bloated after eating, burp, have acid reflux or bad breath, and deal with either loose stools or constipation.

The last pattern feels stuck. You know the gas is there, but it won’t come out until later. TCM connects this with qi deficiency, meaning there may not be enough push to keep things moving. Low energy, poor appetite, and bloating often show up with it.

Follow for the next part, where I’ll show you what to do for each pattern.

## Chinese Translation

你的屁可能比你还了解你的身体。它的气味、声音、热感和出现时机，可能对应很不一样的表现。我正在中国师承学习中医，下面一起看看哪一种更像你。

如果你经常放屁，但几乎不臭，同时还觉得腹胀，中医可能会把这种表现和肝气不舒影响消化联系起来。因为中医认为肝与情绪有关，所以这种情况常在感到压力大或受挫时出现。你还可能觉得烦躁、焦虑或情绪低落，同时伴有口苦或睡眠差。

另一种表现是屁又响、味道又重，有时还带着热感。用中医的话来说，这种表现与湿热有关。食物可能在肠道里停留太久，就像食物一直放在温热的锅里，让细菌有更多时间分解，并产生气味更重的气体。口舌生疮、牙龈肿、疲倦、皮肤出油、小便黄、腹胀、便秘或大便稀也可能同时出现。

还有一种屁几乎没声音，味道却特别重。中医把这种组合和脾胃虚弱、食积联系在一起，就像饭后遇到一场堵车。你可能饭后腹胀、打嗝、反酸或口臭，大便可能偏稀，也可能便秘。

最后一种是气堵着排不出来。你明明感觉它已经在那里，却要过一会儿才能排出。中医把这种表现和气虚联系起来，也就是推动的力量可能不够。身体没劲、食欲差和腹胀也常会一起出现。

关注我，下一篇我会继续讲每种表现分别可以怎么做。
