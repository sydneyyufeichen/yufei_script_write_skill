## Topic

## Video Title

What Causes Period Cramps and Simple Food Remedies

## Caption

Milder period cramps may reflect deficiency rather than blockage. Liver and Kidney deficiency and qi and blood deficiency have different signs and call for different support.

## Spoken Script

Two other period-cramp patterns usually feel milder than the blockage patterns in the last video because they more often come from weakness in the organ systems. In TCM, deficiency means the body doesn’t have enough qi and blood to keep everything nourished and moving. Blockage needs to be opened; deficiency needs to be replenished.

If your lower back aches, fatigue makes it worse, and you’ve become more forgetful, this is the Liver and Kidney deficiency pattern. The ache feels deep and persistent, and your flow is usually lighter and paler. This pattern is often linked with yin deficiency, so the focus is on nourishing the Liver and Kidneys. Make a simple Huangjing and goji tea: simmer 10 grams of Huangjing and 10 grams of goji berries in water, then drink it as tea.

If the pain is dull, your complexion looks pale, and your whole body feels weak and drained, this is the qi and blood deficiency pattern. You may feel too tired to talk. The lower-back ache can linger after your period, and the pale, light flow may take a while to finish. The root may be Spleen deficiency or Kidney deficiency. Poor digestion, a scalloped tongue, and sticky stools point more toward Spleen deficiency. In that case, use Chinese yam, poria, codonopsis, or Bazhen powder to support the Spleen. Hair loss, low spirits, and frequent urination point more toward Kidney deficiency. In that case, eat more black-colored foods such as black sesame, black beans, black rice, or mulberries.

Period cramps need to be addressed according to the pattern. If you’ve made changes and seen no improvement, you may have identified your pattern incorrectly. If you can’t tell which one fits you, leave a comment and I’ll help you take a look.

## Chinese Translation

还有另外两种痛经，它们的痛感不如上一篇讲的淤堵型那么重，更多是由于脏腑的亏虚导致。这里说的亏虚，就是身体的气血不够充足，推动和濡养的力量也会不足。淤堵需要去疏通，而亏虚则需要补。

如果腰酸，劳累后会加重，还可能容易忘事，这就是肝肾亏虚型。这种酸痛绵绵不休，经量通常也偏少、颜色偏淡。肝肾亏虚大部分都是阴虚，这个时候就需要补肝和肾。可以自己煮黄精枸杞汤：用黄精十克、枸杞十克一起煮水代茶饮，很简单。

如果疼痛是隐隐的，平时脸色特别差，感觉整个人没有力气、身体乏力，也不想说话，这就是气血亏虚型。腰酸可能在月经结束后还会持续，经量偏少、颜色偏淡，还有淋漓不尽的感觉。气血亏虚的根源可能是脾虚，也可能是肾虚。如果你消化不好、舌边有齿痕、大便黏，多为脾虚，可以用山药、茯苓、党参或八珍粉来健脾；如果你掉头发、精神萎靡、尿频，则更偏肾虚，可以多吃一些黑色食材，比如黑芝麻、黑豆、黑米和桑葚。

痛经调理一定要先分清类型。做了调整却没有效果，可能是一开始就判断错了。如果分不清自己属于哪一型，可以在评论区留言，我来帮你看看。
