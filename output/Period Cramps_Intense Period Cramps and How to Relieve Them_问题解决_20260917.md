Intense Period Cramps and How to Relieve Them

## Caption

From a TCM perspective, intense period cramps can reflect different blockage patterns. Cold congealing and qi stagnation have different signs, so the routine should match the pattern.

## Spoken Script

From a TCM perspective, intense period pain often points to some kind of blockage in the body. When the channels aren’t flowing freely, pain follows. This is what TCM calls “不通则痛.”

If your lower abdomen feels cold and tight and warmth brings relief, this is cold-congealing blood stasis. Your flow may be light and dark, with deep brown or almost-black clots. For this pattern, focus on driving out cold and staying warm. Use a heat patch, avoid direct air conditioning, and skip iced drinks. Moxibustion is also a useful way to warm the body. Apply it to these three points: Guanyuan is four finger-widths below your navel; Xuehai is three finger-widths above the inner side of your kneecap; and Sanyinjiao is four finger-widths above your inner ankle.

If your flow looks dark purple with clots, your breasts feel sore, and you become noticeably more irritable, this is qi-stagnation blood stasis. You may also feel bloated or tight in the chest and find yourself sighing often. For this pattern, focus on soothing Liver qi and invigorating blood. Slice some fresh ginger and split open the pitted red dates. Add both to a pot with brown sugar and water. Bring it to a boil over high heat, then turn the heat down and simmer for 10 minutes. Start drinking it three days before your period, one bowl a day, and continue until your period ends.

Follow for the next part, where I’ll focus on deficiency-type period cramps, which need a completely different approach from blockage patterns.

## Chinese Translation

在中医看来，经期如果痛得比较严重，多半是体内有淤堵，也就是经络不通导致疼痛，这是我们常说的“不通则痛”。

如果小腹是发冷、发紧的疼，暖一暖会舒服一些，这就是寒凝血瘀型。经量可能偏少、颜色偏暗，还会有深褐色甚至接近黑色的血块。如果是这一型，重点就是祛寒保暖，可以贴暖宝宝，不要对着空调直吹，也不要喝冰的。艾灸是很好的驱寒方法，可以艾灸以下穴位：从肚脐往下量四横指，就是关元；在膝盖内侧上方量三横指找到血海；从内踝往上量四横指找到三阴交。

如果经血偏紫暗、有血块，同时乳房胀痛、脾气明显变急，这就是气滞血瘀型。还可能同时出现腹胀、胸口发闷、总想叹气。如果是这一型，重点就是疏肝活血。把生姜切片，加入去核后掰开的大枣，再放入红糖。所有食材一起放进锅里，加水大火煮开，再转小火煮十分钟。经前三天开始喝，每天一碗，一直喝到经期结束。

关注我，下一篇我会聚焦讲亏虚型痛经，它的表现以及调理思路和淤堵型都完全不一样。
