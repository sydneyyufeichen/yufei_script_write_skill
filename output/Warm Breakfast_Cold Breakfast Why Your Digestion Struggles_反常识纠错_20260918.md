## Topic

Why cold food at breakfast may feel harder to digest, and two ways to balance it.

## Video Title

Cold Breakfast? Why Your Digestion Struggles

## Caption

Cold food can feel harder on digestion first thing in the morning. Warming it up or alternating with cooked breakfasts may make the habit easier on your body.

## Spoken Script

Chinese medicine hates cold food for breakfast. Here's the traditional way to understand why.

In Chinese medicine, cold food is thought to bring cold and dampness into the body. Cold slows digestion, while dampness can feel like heaviness and bloating.

Think of your stomach like a cooking pot that's just starting to warm up in the morning. If the first thing you put in is cold food, it's like pouring cold water into that pot. The temperature drops, so the fire has to work harder to warm everything back up before it can cook the food. Digestion may slow down along with it.

That's why traditional Chinese breakfasts are usually warm and cooked, like congee, eggs, soup, or noodles. The idea is to support digestion instead of hitting it with something cold first thing in the morning.

This doesn't mean everyone has to give up cold food. Some people handle it just fine. But if you already have digestive discomfort, cold food in the morning may come with bloating, loose stools, brain fog, low energy after eating, or a heavy feeling.

If you want to keep cold food in your breakfast, let it come to room temperature and pair it with something warmer, like cooked apples, stewed pears, walnuts, or goji berries. You can also alternate: have cold food one morning and a cooked breakfast the next.

## Chinese Translation

中医不赞成早餐吃冷食。下面用传统中医的视角解释原因。

中医认为，冷食会给身体带来寒湿。寒会减慢消化，湿则可能表现为沉重感和腹胀。

你可以把胃想成一口早晨刚开始升温的锅。如果放进去的第一样东西是冷食，就像往锅里倒了一盆冷水。温度降下来后，锅里的火得先花更多力气把食物重新温热，才能开始烹煮，消化也可能随之慢下来。

这也是为什么传统的中式早餐通常是温热、煮熟的食物，比如粥、鸡蛋、汤或面条。这个思路是帮助消化，而不是一大早先给它一次寒凉刺激。

这并不表示所有人都要戒掉冷食，有些人吃了完全没问题。但如果你本来就有消化方面的困扰，早晨吃冷食可能会带来腹胀、便溏、脑子发蒙、饭后没精神，或者身体有沉重感。

如果你想在早餐里继续吃冷食，可以先放到室温，再搭配煮苹果、炖梨、核桃或枸杞这类偏温暖的食物。也可以隔一天吃冷食，另一天改吃熟的早餐，交替着来。
