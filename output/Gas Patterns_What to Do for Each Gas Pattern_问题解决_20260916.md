## Spoken Script

The smell of your fart isn’t random. It’s your body sending you a signal about what’s going on inside. Here’s what to do for each pattern.

If you pass gas often but it barely smells, and you feel bloated, tap the Gallbladder channel from the sides of your hips, down the outer thighs, to the knees until the area feels warm. Next, massage Qimen, the point just below your ribs. Finish by pushing the Liver channel from the groin down to the knee on both legs until you feel slight warmth. Do the pushing between 7 and 9 p.m., but not right before bed. In TCM, this routine is meant to get constrained qi moving so digestion doesn’t feel stuck in traffic.

If your gas is loud, smells strong, and feels hot, cut back on iced drinks such as ice water, iced coffee, and iced milk. Each day, tap or scrape the Large Intestine channel from the outside of the arm toward the fingers 30 to 50 times. Then tap or scrape the Lung channel from the inside of the arm toward the thumb. In TCM, the Lung and Large Intestine channels are paired, so the routine includes both.

If the gas is quiet but smells terrible, and you have bloating after meals, burping, acid reflux, bad breath, loose stools, or constipation, slow down when you eat. Chew well and stop when you feel about 70 to 80 percent full. Then massage Daling at the center of each wrist for three to five minutes. Find Jianli four finger-widths above the navel. Place both hands there and massage it 100 times clockwise, then 100 times counterclockwise.

If you can feel the gas but can’t pass it, especially when you feel tired, bloated, or have little appetite, try gentle movement such as walking, slow jogging, or Baduanjin. You can pair a circular movement with your breathing: inhale as you circle forward and exhale as you circle backward. The goal is to get qi moving gently, without forcing anything.

## Chinese Translation

屁的气味并不是随机的，而是身体在向你传递内部状况的信号。下面看看每种表现分别可以怎么做。

如果你经常放屁，但几乎不臭，同时还觉得腹胀，可以从臀部两侧沿大腿外侧的胆经敲到膝盖，敲到这个区域微微发热。接着按揉胸下肋骨处的期门穴。最后，从大腿根部沿肝经往下推到膝盖，两条腿都推到微微发热。推的动作可以安排在晚上七点到九点，但不要临睡前做。按照中医的思路，这套动作是为了让郁住的气机重新运行，别让消化一直像堵车一样。

如果屁又响、味道又重，还带着热感，可以减少冰水、冰咖啡和冰牛奶这类冷饮。每天从手臂外侧沿大肠经往手指方向拍打或刮三十到五十次，再从手臂内侧沿肺经往大拇指方向拍打或刮一遍。在中医里，肺经和大肠经互为表里，所以这套动作会同时照顾两条经络。

如果屁声音很小但味道特别重，同时还有饭后腹胀、打嗝、反酸、口臭、大便偏稀或便秘，吃饭时要慢一点。细嚼慢咽，感觉七八分饱时就停下来。然后按揉双手腕中点的大陵穴，各三到五分钟。找到肚脐上方四指的建里穴，双手按住，先顺时针按揉一百圈，再逆时针按揉一百圈。

如果明明感觉屁已经在那里，却一直排不出来，尤其还伴有疲倦、腹胀或食欲差，可以做一些温和的运动，例如散步、慢跑或八段锦。也可以把转圈动作和呼吸配合起来：向前转时吸气，向后转时呼气。目的是温和地带动气机，不要勉强用力。
