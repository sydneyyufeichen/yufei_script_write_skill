## Spoken Script

One warm breakfast doesn't cancel iced drinks all day. I'm studying Chinese medicine in China, and here's the traditional explanation. 

The spleen and stomach are compared to a slow cooker that transforms food into qi and blood. Cold has a constricting quality. Iced drinks, ice cream, and raw salad from the fridge are like pouring cold water into that cooker. Your digestive yang must warm everything before breaking it down. Over time, this is said to weaken that inner fire and the spleen's function, slow qi, and create dampness, which may show up as bloating, loose stools, fatigue, sluggishness, or poor circulation. That's also why cold food is often limited while taking herbal medicine.

Warm or hot water is thought to do the opposite: warm digestion, help transform and transport nutrients, and move waste toward the large intestine. It doesn't need lemon or supplements. For bloating, hard or loose stools, or constipation, one traditional suggestion is to make warm or hot water your only water temperature for two to three days and sip it throughout the day. Some acupuncturists call mental health, digestion, and fertility the top three reasons Americans seek acupuncture, and describe digestion as the easiest to improve, starting with a daily habit.

Many Americans, by contrast, drink ice water all day. In China, people commonly carry thermos bottles and sip warm water in the morning, after meals, and before bed to support digestive warmth, nutrient absorption, circulation, and energy. An iced drink may feel refreshing briefly, but repeated cold is traditionally seen as draining over time.

Does everyone need to quit completely? No. If you have cold hands and feet, weak digestion, coughing after cold drinks, or strong sensitivity to cold food, air, or air conditioning, limiting them may suit you better. If you don't notice those patterns, don't get sick easily, and live somewhere hot and humid, an occasional cold drink can still fit in moderation.

## Chinese Translation

一顿温热早餐抵消不了一整天喝冰饮的影响。我正在中国学习中医师承，下面说说传统中医的解释。

中医常把脾胃比作一口慢炖锅，负责把食物转化成气血。寒具有收引的特性。冰饮、冰淇淋和刚从冰箱拿出来的生菜沙拉，就像往这口锅里倒冷水。消化系统的阳气必须先把它们温热，才能继续分解。传统观点认为，长期如此会削弱这团内在之火和脾的功能，使气机变慢并产生湿，可能表现为腹胀、便溏、疲劳、身体困倦或循环不畅。这也是为什么服用中药期间通常会限制冷食。

温水或热水被认为能起到相反的作用：温暖消化系统，帮助转化和输送营养，并推动废物进入大肠。不需要加柠檬，也不需要补充剂。对于腹胀、大便过硬或过软，或者便秘，一种传统做法是连续两到三天喝水时只选择温水或热水，并在一天中持续小口饮用。一些针灸师把心理健康、消化和生育问题称为美国人寻求针灸的三大原因，并认为消化问题最容易从一个日常习惯开始改善。

相比之下，许多美国人会整天喝冰水。在中国，人们常常随身带着保温杯，在早晨、饭后和睡前喝温水，用这个习惯支持消化所需的温度、营养吸收、循环和能量。冰饮也许能带来短暂的清凉感，但传统观点认为，反复摄入寒凉会随着时间慢慢消耗能量。

那是不是所有人都必须彻底戒掉冷饮？并不是。如果你手脚冰凉、消化较弱，喝冷饮后会咳嗽，或者对冷食、冷空气和空调特别敏感，少喝或不喝可能更适合你。如果你没有这些表现、不容易生病，而且生活在湿热地区，偶尔适量喝冷饮也可以。
