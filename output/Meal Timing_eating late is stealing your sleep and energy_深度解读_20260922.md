## Spoken Script

If you often overwork and skip meals, or if you have irregular eating habits, you need to watch this video. I'm a Chinese medicine apprentice in China, so let's look at what the organ clock says about these habits.

The organ clock divides the day into two-hour windows, each linked to an organ believed to be strongest then. The basic idea is to eat while your digestive fire is more active.

From 7 to 9 a.m. is stomach time, so this framework considers it the best window for breakfast. From 9 to 11 is spleen time. In Chinese medicine, the spleen transforms food into usable energy, so these hours are linked with mental clarity, focus, and steady energy.

From 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. is heart time. Qi and blood are thought to be more abundant in the center of the body, helping digestion. That makes midday another ideal time to eat, when your body may handle lunch most efficiently.

From 5 to 7 p.m. is kidney time. One version uses this as a dinner window that supports recovery without overloading digestion. A stricter pattern in ancient China used only two meals: breakfast from 7 to 9 a.m. and lunch around 1 to 3 p.m., then nothing else. Late eating keeps your body processing food when it should be resting, nighttime focuses on detoxing, tissue repair, and restoring energy. Modern research also examines cellular cleanup, the removal of old or weak cells, in relation to sleep, metabolism, and longevity.

To try the earlier cutoff, go seven days with no food or drinks except water after 7 p.m. Then notice whether your sleep, bloating, digestion, or morning energy changes.

器官时钟把一天分成每两个小时一个时段，并认为每个时段都有一个脏腑系统处于相对旺盛的状态。它的基本思路是，在消化之火较旺盛时进食。

早上 7 点到 9 点是胃最活跃的时段，因此按照这个框架，这是吃早餐的最佳时间。9 点到 11 点是脾最活跃的时段。在中医里，脾负责把食物转化为身体可以使用的能量，因此这段时间也与思路清晰、注意力和稳定的精力有关。

上午 11 点到下午 1 点是心最活跃的时段。此时气血被认为更多地集中在身体中部，能够帮助消化。因此，中午也是一个理想的进食时间，身体可能在这时更有效地处理午餐。

下午 5 点到 7 点是肾最活跃的时段。按照其中一种安排，这段时间可以吃晚餐，让晚餐帮助身体恢复，同时不过度增加消化负担。古代中国还采用过一种更严格的模式：一天只吃两餐，早上 7 点到 9 点吃早餐，下午 1 点到 3 点左右吃午餐，之后不再进食。太晚吃东西会让身体在本该休息时继续处理食物，而夜间原本更专注于排出废物、修复组织和恢复能量。现代研究也会关注细胞清理，也就是清除衰老或状态较弱的细胞，以及这个过程与睡眠、代谢和长寿之间的联系。

如果你想尝试提前停止进食，可以连续七天在晚上 7 点后不再吃东西，也不喝水以外的饮料，然后观察睡眠、腹胀、消化或早晨的精力是否发生变化。
