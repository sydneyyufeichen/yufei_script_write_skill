## Topic

## Video Title

Try This Nose Relief Routine

## Caption

Looking for instant relief from a blocked nose? Try acupressure around the nose first, then move to distal points on the hands and arms.

## Spoken Script

If your nose feels blocked, try this acupressure routine with me. We’ll press the points around your nose first, then move to points farther away on your hands and arms.

First, place both index fingers beside your nostrils. Rub up and down until the area feels warm. Next, find Yingxiang in the small hollows beside the nostrils and massage for three minutes. Move slightly higher to Bitong, just above the nostrils, and massage there too.

Now use your palm to rub from the bridge of your nose up toward Shangxing until the whole path feels warm. To find Shangxing, start at Yintang between your eyebrows, follow the center line to your hairline, then move one thumb-width higher.

At the top of your head, find the small hollow about five finger-widths above the hairline, or directly above the tips of your ears. This is Baihui. Massage it ten circles clockwise, then ten circles counterclockwise.

For the farther points, press Yemen between your ring finger and little finger for three minutes. Then press Hegu at the web between your thumb and index finger. Bend your elbow and find Quchi near the outside of the elbow. Once you feel soreness, press a little deeper for 30 seconds.

## Chinese Translation

如果你正被鼻塞困扰，可以跟我做一遍这套穴位按揉。我们先按鼻子周围的穴位，再按手部和手臂上的远端穴。

先把双手食指放在鼻翼两侧，上下来回摩擦，直到微微发热。接着找到鼻翼两侧凹陷处的迎香穴，按揉三分钟。然后稍微往上，找到鼻翼上方的鼻通穴，也进行按揉。

接下来，用手掌从鼻根向上搓到上星穴，直到整条路线微微发热。找上星穴时，先从两眉之间的印堂穴开始，沿正中线向上找到前发际线，再往上一个拇指宽的位置。

头顶还有一个小凹陷，大约在前发际线上五指的位置，也可以从两侧耳尖向上找，这里是百会穴。先顺时针揉十圈，再逆时针揉十圈。

远端穴位先按液门穴，它在无名指和小拇指的指缝处，按三分钟。然后按合谷穴，它在拇指和食指之间的虎口处。接着屈肘，在手肘外侧附近找到曲池穴。按到有酸痛感后，再稍微加力按三十秒。
